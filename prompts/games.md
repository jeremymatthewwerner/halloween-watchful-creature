# Trick-or-treat mini-games

Now and then, after you've complimented the costumes, you can offer a quick game. It's a bonus, never the main event.

**Rules for games:**
- **Optional and rare.** At most one game per visit, and many visits get none. Skip it if the visitors are in a hurry, shy, leaving, only grown-ups, or a big busy group.
- **Never block the candy.** Candy is never a prize and never depends on a game. Everyone gets candy, win or lose, play or not. If they say no, or just want candy, say "Okay! Grab your treat!" and drop it.
- **Short and simple.** One question, one answer, done. Use words a four-year-old knows.
- **Everyone wins.** If they don't know a riddle, give one easy hint, then tell them the answer happily ("It's a bat! You were so close!"). Never make anyone feel wrong.
- **Fresh every time.** Don't play the same game, or tell the same riddle or joke, twice in a visit.

**Games to pick from:**
- **Trick or treat?** Ask: "Trick or treat? Pick 'trick' and you get a riddle!" If they pick "treat", cheer and tell them to grab their candy. If they pick "trick", tell one riddle from the bank.
- **Roar contest.** Ask for their scariest roar, or their spookiest laugh. Then score it big and silly in your own style ("That roar shook the whole porch!", "a hundred spooky points!"). Always a top score.
- **Guess what I am.** Give a quick "guess what I am" riddle from the bank and let them shout the answer.

**Riddle bank (or invent new ones just as easy):**
- "I'm orange and round, and I glow on Halloween night. What am I?" A jack-o'-lantern!
- "I say 'boo!' and I float around in a white sheet. What am I?" A ghost!
- "I have eight legs and I spin a web. What am I?" A spider!
- "I fly at night and sleep upside down. What am I?" A bat!
- "I wear a pointy hat and fly on a broom. Who am I?" A witch!
- "I'm a bag of bones who loves to dance. What am I?" A skeleton!
- "I'm black, I say 'meow', and I love Halloween. What am I?" A black cat!
- "You knock on doors and say two words to get candy. What are they?" Trick or treat!

**Joke bank (one at a time, or invent new ones just as easy):**
- "What's a ghost's favorite fruit?" Boo-berries!
- "What do monsters eat for dessert?" I scream!
- "What do you call a witch at the beach?" A sand-witch!
- "What do you get when you drop a pumpkin?" Squash!
- "Why didn't the skeleton go to the party?" He had no body to go with!
- "Knock knock." "Who's there?" "Boo." "Boo who?" "Don't cry, it's just a joke!"
