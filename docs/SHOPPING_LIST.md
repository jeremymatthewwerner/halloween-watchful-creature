# Shopping list: Pumpkin Critic (from Burlingame, CA)

All the stores are on one southbound run down 101 / El Camino. Do it in this order:

| # | Store | Address | Drive from Burlingame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Michaels (San Mateo) | 2925 S El Camino Real, San Mateo | ~10 min |
| 2 | Central Computers (San Mateo) | 2727 S El Camino Real, San Mateo · 650-345-5888 | ~10 min, a block from Michaels |
| 3 | Home Depot (San Mateo) | 2001 Chess Dr, San Mateo | ~10 min |
| 4 | Best Buy (San Carlos) | 1127 Industrial Rd, San Carlos | ~12 min |
| (5) | Micro Center (Santa Clara), **only if Central Computers is out** | 5201 Stevens Creek Blvd, Santa Clara | ~30 min (more in traffic) |

**Call Central Computers first (650-345-5888)** and ask about everything in section 2 below. If they have it all, skip Micro Center.

Hours and stock change daily. Before you go, check "Pick up today" on bestbuy.com and the in-store stock on microcenter.com with the store selected, or call the store.

**Closer alternatives:**
- Michaels Colma (4925 Junipero Serra Blvd)
- Best Buy Colma (200 Colma Blvd)
- Home Depot San Carlos (1125 Old County Rd)

## 1. Michaels: the creature body
- [ ] **Hollow foam craft pumpkin, 12–18"** (the "carvable" kind). It must be big enough for the speakerphone to fit inside (~5" across).
- [ ] **Black tulle or black speaker mesh fabric.** It goes behind the carved mouth so the camera and speaker are hidden but can still see and be heard.
- [ ] **Critic accessories:** a small beret or chef's toque, a monocle or round glasses, and a bow tie. Doll and costume aisles have these. Spirit Halloween is a backup.
- [ ] Hot glue gun and glue sticks, plus a craft knife (skip these if you have them).

## 2. Central Computers (San Mateo): the body controller (and anything Best Buy lacks)
I confirmed online that they stock Raspberry Pi. The hobby parts below are probably in their maker/DIY section, but **call to confirm**. They can transfer stock from other stores.
- [ ] **Arduino Uno (or Uno-compatible) starter kit** that includes an **SG90 servo** and a **5V relay module**, plus a breadboard and jumper wires. If no kit has both, buy a bare Uno, a breadboard and jumpers, 1× SG90 servo and 1× 5V relay module separately.
- [ ] **3× NeoPixel Jewel 7 (Adafruit #2226)** or any WS2812B 7-LED jewel or 8–12 LED ring. Two are the eyes and one is a spare.
- [ ] **Active (repeater) USB 3 extension cable, 16 ft.**
- [ ] **Powered USB 3 hub**, if cheaper or better than Best Buy's.
- [ ] **Soldering iron kit and solder** (~$20), unless you have one.
- [ ] Optional: 22 AWG stranded hookup wire (~10 ft).
- [ ] Optional: **Raspberry Pi 5 8GB kit** (board, 27W supply, case with cooler, 64GB+ microSD), if cheaper than Best Buy's CanaKit.

## 3. Home Depot: light and power
- [ ] **Warm LED clamp light or small LED flood** (an orange or amber bulb is fine). Aim it at where visitors stand. **The camera can't judge costumes in the dark**, so this item matters a lot.
- [ ] Outdoor-rated extension cord (25 ft) and a power strip.
- [ ] Zip ties, gaffer or duct tape, and a few strips of Velcro.

## 4. Best Buy (San Carlos): the senses
- [ ] **Logitech C920s Pro HD webcam**, ~$60–70. [bestbuy.com](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/searchpage.jsp?id=pcat17071&st=logitech+c920s)
- [ ] **Anker PowerConf speakerphone**, ~$110–140. It has 6 mics and echo cancellation; use it over USB, not Bluetooth. [bestbuy.com](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/anker-powerconf-bluetooth-speakerphone-conference-speaker-black/6421726.p?skuId=6421726)
- [ ] **Powered USB 3 hub, 4+ ports, with its own power adapter.** Any brand works (Anker, Insignia, Belkin).
- [ ] **USB-C to USB-A adapter**, if your Mac only has USB-C.
- [ ] **CanaKit Raspberry Pi 5 8GB Starter MAX kit**, ~$180, for Pi mode later. It includes the power supply, case, cooler and preloaded SD card. [bestbuy.com](https://www.bestbuy.com/product/canakit-raspberry-pi-5-starter-max-kit-8gb-turbine-white/JXS4YQC29W/sku/6580288)

## 5. Micro Center (Santa Clara): backup only
Go here only if Central Computers can't supply the section 2 items. Its stock is confirmed online, and it can also cover anything Best Buy was out of: the webcam, hub and Pi kit.
- [ ] **Elegoo UNO R3 "Most Complete" starter kit**, ~$58, **in-store pickup only**. It includes the Arduino, USB cable, breadboard, jumper wires, an **SG90 servo** (jaw mode) and a **5V relay** (animatronic mode). [microcenter.com](https://www.microcenter.com/product/704602/elegoo-uno-r3-project-most-complete-starter-kit-with-tutorial-compatible-with-arduino-ide-(200-components))
- [ ] **3× Adafruit NeoPixel Jewel, 7 × RGB** (part #2226), ~$6 each. Two are the eyes and one is a spare. [microcenter.com](https://www.microcenter.com/product/456218/adafruit-industries-neopixel-jewel-7-x-ws2812-5050-rgb-led-with-integrated-drivers)
- [ ] **Active (repeater) USB 3 extension cable, 16 ft.** It lets the Mac stay inside while the hub sits at the pumpkin.
- [ ] **Basic soldering iron kit and solder** (~$20), unless you have one. The Jewels need three short wires soldered on.
- [ ] Optional: 22 AWG stranded hookup wire, about 10 ft, for running the eye wires inside the pumpkin.

## Online backups (if a store is out)
Nothing on this list is online-only, since Central Computers or Micro Center should have everything. If a store is out, order these instead. They aren't needed until the "glowing eyes" step, so next-day delivery is fine.
- NeoPixel Jewel 7 (#2226): [adafruit.com/product/2226](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2226)
- Elegoo UNO R3 starter kit: [Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=elegoo+uno+r3+super+starter+kit). Check that the listing includes an "SG90 servo" and a "5V relay".
- Active USB 3 extension, 16 ft: [Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=active+usb+3.0+extension+cable+16ft)
- Powered USB 3 hub: [Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=powered+usb+3.0+hub+4+port)
- Louder speakerphone upgrade (Jabra Speak2 55): [Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=jabra+speak2+55)

## Not buying (parked)
- **Store-bought animatronic.** I found no friendly 2026 model that's confirmed to work with an external trigger. The friendly ones have their own voices, which would talk over ours. The relay support is still in the firmware for later.
